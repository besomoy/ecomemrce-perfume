The Art of Memory

Scent & Aura began as a quiet whisper in the crowded streets of Paris. Founded by visionary perfumer Jean-Luc Varin, our maison was built on a single, unwavering belief: that perfume is not merely an accessory, but a liquid memory.

For over a century, we have traveled the globe in search of the rarest ingredients—from the misty oud forests of Southeast Asia to the rose valleys of Bulgaria. Every bottle that leaves our atelier is hand-poured, hand-labeled, and imbued with the soul of its creator. We do not just make scents; we craft legacies.

Our Philosophy

Pillars of Excellence

Rare Ingredients

We source only the finest natural absolutes and essences, prioritizing quality over cost and rarity over volume.

Master Craftsmanship

Our "Noses" are artists of the invisible, blending traditional French techniques with modern molecular precision.

Sustainable Future

We are committed to ethical sourcing, refillable packaging, and preserving biodiversity for future generations.

The Alchemist

Antoine Dubois

"Perfume is the shadow of the soul."

Born in Grasse to a family of rose growers, Antoine possesses a unique synesthesia that allows him to "hear" scents as musical chords. His creations are not linear; they are symphonies. After training under the legendary masters of the 20th century, he founded Scent & Aura to break the rules of classical perfumery.
