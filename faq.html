The longevity of a perfume depends on several factors, including skin type, humidity, and the concentration of the fragrance. Dry skin absorbs perfume quickly, so we recommend moisturizing before application. Also, applying to pulse points (wrist, neck, behind ears) helps diffuse the scent throughout the day.

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email containing a tracking number. You can use this number on our website under the "Track Order" section or directly on the courier's website to monitor your shipment.

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unopened and unused items in their original packaging. Please note that opened perfumes cannot be returned due to hygiene and health regulations. If you received a damaged item, please contact our support team immediately.
