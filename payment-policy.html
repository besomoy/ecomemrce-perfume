Payment Policy

Accepted Methods

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and select local payment methods depending on your region.

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay (supported devices)

Billing and Verification

Orders may be subject to verification. Ensure your billing details match the cardholder information to avoid delays.

Security

Transactions are encrypted and processed through secure payment gateways. We do not store full card details on our servers.

Charges and Currencies

Charges appear in USD unless otherwise stated at checkout. International customers may see additional conversion fees from their card issuer.
