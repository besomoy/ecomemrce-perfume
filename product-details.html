Vampire Blood

(12 Customer Reviews)

A daring confluence of blood orange and rich saffron, grounded by the earthy depth of cedarwood. Vampire Blood is not for the faint of heart—it is a statement of power, mystery, and eternal elegance.

Size

SKU: SA-003-VB

Category: Oriental Spice

Tags: Unisex, Luxury

Vampire Blood is an olfactory masterpiece that transcends time. Inspired by the legends of the night, it opens with a sharp, invigorating burst of blood orange and spicy saffron, instantly capturing attention. As it settles, the heart reveals a velvety blend of rose absolute and jasmine sambac, adding a floral richness that is both seductive and sophisticated.

The base is where the true character lies—grounded in smoky oud, warm amber, and creamy sandalwood. It leaves a trail that is unforgettable, a lingering whisper of mystery that follows you wherever you go. Encased in our signature crimson bottle, it is as much a visual delight as it is a sensory one.
