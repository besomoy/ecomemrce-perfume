Inside the Paris Atelier

Behind every Scent & Aura fragrance is a ritual of craftsmanship. Our Paris atelier blends traditional techniques with modern precision to capture memories in liquid form.

From the first maceration to the final bottling, each step is handled by artisans who understand the delicate balance of light, depth, and longevity.

The Ritual of Blending

Our perfumers begin with raw essences, selecting notes that build a story on the skin. Citrus lifts, resins ground, and florals weave through the heart of the blend.

"Perfume should be worn like a memory - close, intimate, and unforgettable."

Materials with Meaning

We partner with farms that value heritage cultivation. Every ingredient is selected for its character, sustainability, and the way it evolves in composition.
